The 1-click Gmail button

Send a trial email straight to Trial Trap, no copy-paste.

Drag this button up to your browser's bookmarks bar. Then, whenever you're reading a free-trial email in Gmail, click it: Trial Trap opens with that email already filled in.

📩 Trials from this email ← drag me to your bookmarks bar

🔒 No password, no sign-in. The email only travels from your Gmail tab to Trial Trap on your own computer. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere else.

How to set it up

  1. Make sure your bookmarks bar is showing (in Chrome or Edge: Ctrl+Shift+B).
  2. Drag the green 📩 Trials from this email button above onto that bar.
  3. Start Trial Trap as usual (npm start), then open a free-trial email in Gmail and click the button.

It opens Trial Trap at localhost:3000. If you changed the port, use that number instead (see the fallback below).
